About

I am a lecturer and researcher in Computer Science and Engineering. My work applies machine learning and deep learning to healthcare problems such as heart disease, kidney disease, dyslipidemia, and skin lesion segmentation. I also work on privacy-preserving federated learning for IoT and network security. I supervise undergraduate research and advise students on academics and career planning.
